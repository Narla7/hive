# Pending work

Tracked deliberately rather than left implicit. Anything here is **not** covered
by the suite, and the suite is currently green — so nothing here is silently
protected.

## Deferred: tests

The dataset work landed with its tests. These are the gaps I know about and did
not close, in priority order.

### 1. `BrokerConfig` cost parameters are untested against anything realistic
`fee_bps`, `slippage_bps`, `impact_bps`, `max_participation` are exercised only
by incidental assertions. There is no test pinning how a strategy's return
responds to doubling costs, which is the cheapest available test of whether an
edge is real. A genuine edge degrades gracefully; a fake one vanishes.

**Why it matters:** this is the single highest-value missing test in the project.
Worth adding as `--cost-sensitivity` on the CLI.

### 2. `FileMarket` header parsing is untested on malformed provenance
The `#` comment skip works, but nothing asserts behaviour for a file with no
header, a header with a BOM, or CRLF line endings.

### 3. `SessionSpan.indices` beyond its one caller
`indices(offset, step, count)` is tested once. `SessionPlan.validate`'s
overlap detection is tested by constructing an overlap directly rather than via
a config that produces one.

### 4. Buy-and-hold on a degenerate span
`buy_and_hold` returns zeros when no window has ≥2 bars. Not asserted.

### 5. `windows_for_gen` with `window_stride` explicitly overridden
The override path (`window_stride != 0`) is never exercised. Only the derived
default is tested, which is the easy case.

## Deferred: accuracy, and why

These are known-false behaviours, not oversights. Listed so they are not
rediscovered as surprises.

### 1. No bid/ask. `SimulatedMarket.spread_bps` is stored and never read
Fills happen at `close ± slippage` with no quote. Every result therefore assumes
free liquidity. Adding a real quote is the single largest accuracy win
available and touches only `broker.py`.

### 2. Intrabar stop/target resolution is arbitrary
Bars are OHLC. When a bar's range contains both the stop and the target, the
fill is taken at `bar.close`. In a real market which level is touched first is
unknowable from OHLC, and it decides the trade. For a strategy built entirely on
`stop_loss`/`take_profit` this is the dominant remaining error. Fix: sample an
intrabar Brownian-bridge path, and offer a pessimistic mode that always assumes
the stop fills when both are touched.

### 3. Fills execute at the close of a bar the agent never observed
`decide()` sees `bars[:i]` and fills at `bars[i].close`. Not a look-ahead leak
— the agent has strictly less information than a real one — but mechanically
wrong. Real sequence: signal from bar *i*'s close, fill at bar *i+1*'s open.

### 4. The committed dataset is synthetic
`data/progeny-1min.csv` is generated by `tools/build_dataset.py`. A holdout
measured on it demonstrates generalisation across independent synthetic
sessions and **nothing** about real markets. The winning strategy found real
structure in the *generator's* regime switching, which is an artifact of
`REGIMES` in that file.

No free, no-key, licence-clean 1-minute source was reachable. Stooq was the
candidate and sits behind a JavaScript proof-of-work challenge — an anti-bot
control, and building a bypass into a committed tool is not a trade worth making.

Swapping in real bars is mechanical: same schema
(`symbol,ts,open,high,low,close,volume`), naive local timestamps, RTH only. The
real-bar contract is in the README.

### 5. The reported holdout is not independent of drift
A long-only strategy on an asset that trended over the sample makes money on any
later window. This is why `buy_and_hold` is now printed beside every holdout
result. It is a partial control, not a full one: it does not adjust for the
strategy's beta to the market.

### 6. Regulatory state changed recently
FINRA repealed the pattern-day-trader rule effective 4 June 2026 (Notice 26-10);
the $25,000 minimum and the PDT designation are gone, replaced by intraday
margin standards tied to actual exposure. Modelling a $25k gate now would be
wrong. The replacement constraint is not implemented at all.

## Deferred: search

### The search does not usually beat its hand-written prior
Measured across market seeds: never worse on 6/6, better on 1/6. The seeds were
written after inspecting signal distributions from the generator, so they sit
near the optimum. The defensible claim is *"evolution adds to the human prior and
never subtracts from it"*, not *"evolution finds strategies people miss."*

Demonstrating the stronger claim needs a market where the prior is not already
good — which is item 4 above.
